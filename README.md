# pmm-competitive-analysis
## Competitive Intelligence Claude Skill

An AI-assisted research skill designed to help marketers and product marketing professionals conduct structured competitive research, evaluate market evidence and turn findings into actionable insights.

## Overview

Competitive research often involves gathering information from multiple sources, assessing competitor claims, identifying patterns and translating findings into strategic recommendations.

This custom Claude Skill provides a structured approach to that process. It guides Claude through competitor research, analysis and synthesis, with an emphasis on evidence quality, research transparency and practical outputs.

## What It Helps You Do

The skill supports several stages of competitive intelligence:

| Capability | What it does |
|---|---|
| Competitor Research | Structures research into competitor products, capabilities, positioning and target customers. |
| Competitive Benchmarking | Compares competitors against relevant criteria and buyer considerations. |
| SWOT Analysis | Identifies and prioritises strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats. |
| Voice of Customer | Analyses customer reviews to identify recurring themes and potential buying concerns. |
| Strategic Recommendations | Translates research findings into prioritised actions with suggested owners and metrics. |
| Sales Battlecards | Turns competitive insights into practical talking points for sales conversations. |

## How It Works

The skill follows a structured research process:

1. **Define the scope:** Identify the product, competitors, target market and buyer alternatives.
2. **Gather evidence:** Collect information from relevant public sources.
3. **Evaluate findings:** Assess source quality, information freshness and evidence limitations.
4. **Analyse competitors:** Compare positioning, capabilities, customer feedback and competitive advantages.
5. **Develop insights:** Identify meaningful patterns, strategic implications and areas of opportunity.
6. **Create deliverables:** Organise findings into SWOT analyses, competitive benchmarks, recommendations or sales battlecards.
